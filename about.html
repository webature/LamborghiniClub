About The Club

Embrace the Lamborghini essence with the UK Club, uniting enthusiasts in celebration of an iconic legacy.

The rationale of the club is simple – to celebrate and appreciate the most outrageous, exciting, and exotic car marque in the world. Lamborghini Club UK is an enthusiasts club run by enthusiasts for enthusiasts.

We are open to all, regardless of ownership and age, who share a passion for the Lamborghini marque and who wish to socialise with like-minded people.

While there has been a club over many years in various forms, our current iteration was officially formed in May 1987. The club operates through a limited company, hosting an Annual General Meeting and filing annual audited accounts with Companies House.

Membership Privileges

Events & Gatherings

  • Welcome pack upon joining.
  • Meeting with other Lamborghini owners and enthusiasts at a variety of events, some exclusive, both within the UK and Europe ranging from static display shows to driving trips including track days.
  • An array of regional events organized by volunteer members covering eight regions.
  • Hospitality at our official club events using the LC UK trailer.

Community & Network

  • Invitations to join one or more of our WhatsApp groups, categorized by regional areas and model range.
  • Access to a vast library of technical information and knowledge.

Publications & Media

  • Free copy of the Club’s quarterly full-colour newsletter – Rivista Veloce.
  • Regular E-Bull email bulletins keeping you up to date.
  • Free copy of the Club’s full-colour calendar.

Support & Savings

  • Access to the Club’s unique insurance scheme with Locktons.
  • Free documentation when bringing a Lamborghini into the UK for the first time.
  • Benefit from discounts exclusive to Club members with official UK dealers for servicing, parts, and official Lamborghini merchandise.
  • Exclusive discounts with various suppliers for a variety of items and services.

(For full details refer to the benefits page in a copy of the Rivista Veloce)
